Redline Garage - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Redline Garage is a racing game. We respect your privacy and keep it simple.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Data on your device

Your game progress (cash, cars, upgrades, records and settings) is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as ads or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
