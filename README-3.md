# Redline Garage 3D

A 3D drag racing and car tuning game that runs in the browser, built with Three.js.

## Features

- 16 original cars (no real brands), each with its own body shape: from a muscle car and a pickup to a track flat-6, V10 and V12 supercars, a V12 super-SUV, twin-turbo, V12 and electric hypercars, a quad-turbo W16 and a hybrid V12 megacar
- Tuning: paint, 7 rim styles, rim and caliper colors, spoilers, front bumpers, hoods, ride height, window tint and underglow
- Performance upgrades: engine, tires, turbo, gearbox and weight reduction
- 5 tracks: quarter-mile drags at City Nights, Ocean Drive and Desert Run, a half-mile runway at the Airfield, and Stunt Park, a 680 m lap with crests, banked hairpins and a vertical loop
- Career: beat a crew of 5 racers and a boss on each track to unlock the next one
- Manual gearbox with PERFECT shifts and launches, skill and win-streak bonuses, daily reward, daily race, weekly events and best-time records
- Setup tuning per car: gearing, tire pressure, launch RPM and nitro (the best setup is different for every car)
- Ghost mode: race a replay of your own best run on each track
- Rain on City Nights (less grip, bigger prize), photo-finish slow motion on close races
- 25 achievements with cash rewards, car rarity tiers (Common to Legendary)
- Photo mode: camera angles, filters, save or share the shot
- Christmas tree start, nitro with exhaust fire and sparks, chase and side cameras, crowds in the stands
- Engine sounds built from a simulated exhaust (cylinder pulses, pipes and muffler) for each engine type, plus crowd sounds and 5 background music tracks (no audio files)

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repository
(Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → main) and open the link it gives you.

## Controls

- Tap **GAS** (or the screen) when the light turns green
- Tap **SHIFT** when the needle reaches the green zone for a perfect shift
- **Nitro** once per race
- Keyboard: Space for GAS and SHIFT, N for nitro, C to switch camera
- Garage: drag the car to spin it, hold **Hold to rev** to rev the engine

## Before launch

`TEST_ALL` in `index.html` unlocks every car for free while testing. Set it to `false` for release.
